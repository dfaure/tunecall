TuneCall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

TuneCall is an offline viewer for your own scanned fake-book PDFs: it lets you search those books by song title and opens them at the right page. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Short version

TuneCall does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

  • Your PDF books — you add them yourself. They are stored only in the app's own folder on your device and are never uploaded or shared.
  • Song indexes and setlists — small files listing song titles and page numbers, and any setlists you create. These stay on your device.

All of this remains on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network use

The app uses the Internet for one purpose only: when you tap Reload, it downloads public song-index files (a manifest and per-book .db index files) from the developer's server. These files contain only song titles and page numbers — no personal data. Nothing about you or your device is sent to the server; it is a plain download. If you never tap Reload, the app makes no network connections.

Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, used for the index download described above. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or shared storage.

Children

TuneCall is a general-purpose music utility. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
