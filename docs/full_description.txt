TuneCall is a fast song finder for musicians who play from scanned fake books and real books.

Type a few letters of a tune's title and TuneCall searches across all your books at once, then opens the matching PDF straight to the correct page — no flipping, no guessing, no fumbling on a gig or at a session.

KEY FEATURES

• Instant search across every indexed book at once
• Jumps directly to the exact scanned page for each song
• Setlists: build, reorder, and play through a set page by page
• Pinch to zoom and pan — crop into the staff you need
• Works fully offline once your PDFs are installed
• Clean, full-screen page viewer made for the music stand

BRING YOUR OWN BOOKS

TuneCall does NOT include any songbooks or sheet music. It is a viewer for PDF files you already own. You copy your own scanned fake-book PDFs onto the device over USB, and TuneCall makes them searchable.

When you tap Reload, the app downloads small song-index files (just a list of titles and page numbers) for supported books, so search knows where each tune lives. The PDFs themselves always stay on your device.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Copy your songbook PDFs to the app's folder via USB.
2. Tap Reload to fetch the matching song indexes.
3. Start typing a title — and play.

WHO IT'S FOR

Jazz and gigging musicians, music students, and anyone who reads from large collections of scanned charts and is tired of scrolling through hundreds of pages to find one tune.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Just your music, found fast.
